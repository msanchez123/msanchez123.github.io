Sunset Hop

Endless bhop course, built on Source-engine movement quirks. The route climbs, drops, folds back on itself, and mixes reachable jumps with grapple gaps. Glowing boost pads change your next jump height, while obstacles force mid-air dodges.

W A S D move  ·  Mouse look  ·  hold Space to auto-bhop
A/D + mouse in air = speed gain, no cap  ·  Ctrl/C crouch
Click/E hold = grapple rope (swing, release to fling)  ·  Shift dash in look direction (recharges)  ·  R reset to checkpoint  ·  fall = back to last platform

Crouching halves your hitbox from the bottom up — duck under low slabs to reach ledges you can't stand-jump onto.

Sensitivity1.00

Multiplayer — same room code = same course, and you see each other.
